# Animal avatar sources

The 31 animal avatars are selected Twemoji SVG graphics downloaded from the
official Twemoji repository on 2026-07-14.

- Source: https://github.com/twitter/twemoji/tree/master/assets/svg
- Graphics license: CC BY 4.0
- License text: https://github.com/twitter/twemoji/blob/master/LICENSE-GRAPHICS
- Attribution: Copyright 2019 Twitter, Inc and other contributors

Included animals: cat, dog, fox, bear, panda, koala, tiger, lion, cow, pig,
frog, monkey, chicken, penguin, bird, chick, owl, unicorn, bee, butterfly,
octopus, turtle, dolphin, and whale.
The expanded selection also includes mouse, rabbit, raccoon, giraffe, zebra,
hedgehog, and kangaroo.
